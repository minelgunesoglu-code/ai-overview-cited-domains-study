## Where the data comes from

The rows were written by hand on 10 June 2026 while reading the source panel of
Google's AI Overview for each search. No page was fetched, mirrored or archived.
The data holds domain names and short notes, not page content.

## What this licence does NOT cover

`cited_domains`, and the names and titles quoted in `raw_note` (for example
"Zapier +3" or the name of a review outlet), identify third-party publishers.
They are reproduced because the study is about which publishers an AI Overview
cites. The author makes no claim of ownership over them and does not license
them.
